Hi, hello, welcome! If you're reading this, you've found the brand-new home of
**Katherine's Absurdities** — and I'm so glad you're here.

For a long time my creative life has been scattered across a dozen places: jewelry in one
spot, paintings in another, event photos buried in my camera roll, ideas on sticky notes
everywhere. This little corner of the internet is my attempt to finally gather all of it
under one (very pink) roof.

## What you'll find here

- **Absurd Jewelry** — handmade, one-of-a-kind pieces, restocked whenever inspiration strikes.
- **A portfolio** of my art and the events I've helped bring to life.
- **This blog**, where I'll share studio notes, new collections, and the occasional happy mess.

## What's coming

This is very much a *version one*. Down the road I want to add easy checkout, custom
commission request forms, and event booking — but I didn't want to wait for "perfect" to
say hello.

So: poke around, follow along on Instagram, and don't be shy about reaching out. Making
things for people I've never met is genuinely my favorite part of all this.

Thanks for being here. Let's make something absurd.

— Katherine
